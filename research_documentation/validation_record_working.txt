FINAL TORQUE-TO-CURVATURE ADMITTANCE REGRESSION
Date: 2026-09-21
The raw body-torque and temporary force-spring implementations were rejected
because they produced nonuniform curvature dependent on gravity and effective
joint inertia. The final implementation maps commanded torque to a uniformly
distributed equilibrium bend: phi = 180 degrees * torque / 0.010 N*m.

INTERMEDIATE EXTENDED TORQUE RANGE (SUPERSEDED BY DIRECT RUNTIME JOINTS)
Date: 2026-09-21
The tip-phi calculation was changed from one shortest-path base-to-tip
quaternion to the sum of the five local inter-disc bend vectors. This permits
an accumulated bend greater than 180 degrees to be reported without wrapping.
X/Y D6 limits were increased from +/-40 to +/-80 degrees.

Diagnostic requests for 270 and 360 degrees exposed a repeatable constraint
ceiling near 52 degrees in the serial A/B intervals; they reached about 262.3
and 282.2 degrees and were rejected as working limits. Collision filtering,
increased drive gains, and asymmetric A/B targets did not remove that ceiling
and were not retained.

DIRECT-INTERVAL 270-DEGREE TORQUE REGRESSION
Date: 2026-09-24
Cause of the prior ceiling: each later interval used two serial A/B D6
constraints. Those intervals stopped near 52 degrees even when their targets
were increased. Collision filtering, increased gains, and asymmetric split
targets did not remove the constraint and were not retained.

Current runtime correction: before PhysX initialization, the loader deactivates
the eight authored A/B constraints and creates one direct D6 joint between each
adjacent disc pair. Existing A/B ROS topics remain compatible: their two
half-angle vectors are summed into the direct joint's full interval target.
The unused virtual links remain as kinematic compatibility objects.
