2026-09-24 - Full-launch sensor-publication regression
------------------------------------------------------
The graphical Isaac Sim launch exited after initialization because the sensor
publisher referenced station_orientation without assigning it. Restored the
base-to-station relative-quaternion calculation before publishing each station
orientation. The bend verifier now also invokes publish_sensor_readings(), so
pose, strain, bridge-voltage, and station-orientation publication errors are
covered by the automated simulation check.

The corrected installed loader subsequently remained active through a
35-second headless full-entry-point test. The timeout exposed a separate
shutdown race in which a final sensor message could be published after the ROS
context was invalidated. The main loop now checks rclpy.ok() at entry and again
after rendering, and shutdown is requested only for a still-valid context.
A repeated 20-second installed-loader test loaded the six-disc USD, entered the
simulation loop, published sensor data, and terminated at the intentional
timeout without a Python traceback. The expanded verifier also passed at
0.005 N*m: expected phi 90.0000 degrees, measured range
90.0000--90.0000 degrees, tracking error 0.0000 degrees.
2026-09-24 - Synchronized whole-limb bending transient
------------------------------------------------------
The four non-base physical intervals each combine compatibility commands A and
B on one direct runtime joint. Both halves were previously slew-limited at the
full 20 deg/s, so their sum changed at 40 deg/s while the base interval changed
at 20 deg/s. This created a nonuniform, visibly segment-wise transient even
though the final equilibrium was uniform. Each A/B half is now limited to
10 deg/s, making all five physical interval targets advance together at
20 deg/s. The Isaac verifier now fails if the five transient X/Y drive targets
differ after the first controller update.

2026-09-24 - Moderate synchronized speed increase
--------------------------------------------------
The effective physical-interval target slew rate was increased from 20 deg/s
to 30 deg/s. Each A/B compatibility half now advances at 15 deg/s, so its
vector sum remains synchronized with the base interval at 30 deg/s. This is a
50 percent transition-speed increase without returning to the earlier
two-to-one rate mismatch.
An eight-second Isaac regression at a 180-degree position request reported
identical first-step physical targets [0.0, 0.125] degrees on all five joints,
a final measured range of 179.9999--179.9999 degrees, and interval bends of
approximately 36 degrees each. VERIFY RESULT: PASS.
2026-09-24 - Bridge graph visibility above 1 V
------------------------------------------------
The voltage trace disappeared when extreme compression made the ideal active
gauge resistance nonpositive and bridge_model_working.py correctly published
NaN. Matplotlib breaks lines at NaN. The voltage plot now renders non-finite
samples at a dashed +1.25 V display boundary with a warning, while preserving
NaN on ROS and false bridge_valid status. Voltage-axis minimum padding was
reduced from 1.0 V to 0.02 V so finite changes above 1 V remain visible.
2026-09-24 - Actuator-scale torque-command interface
----------------------------------------------------
The calibrated admittance command was rescaled by 100: 0.1/0.5/1.0/1.5 N*m
now request 18/90/180/270 degrees, with a +/-1.5 N*m software limit and
2.0 N*m/s slew limit. This preserves the same simulated equilibrium behavior
while expressing the input over approximately 0--15.3 kgf*cm. It does not
convert the controller to raw torque or validate a physical actuator/material
moment-curvature relationship. Rescaled Isaac regressions are pending below.
